# The Inside Man — launch notes

Initial playable preview includes:

- 4–10 player private rooms
- account-required hosting with guest QR/code joining
- one persistent hidden Inside Man per match
- 60 launch scenarios across survival, sci-fi, mystery, everyday, sports, fantasy, comedy, and adventure themes
- five missions per match
- private Crew side objectives and Inside Man sabotage objectives
- private group voting with automatic mission resolution
- Crew and sabotage progress meters (first to three)
- one-use player accusations
- private Guilty / Not Guilty trials
- immediate Crew victory for correctly convicting the Inside Man
- sabotage penalty for wrong convictions
- no player elimination
- final role reveal, private-objective points, and replay

Future packs can move scenario content into normalized catalog tables without changing the room protocol.
