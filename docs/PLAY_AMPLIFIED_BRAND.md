# Play Amplified — Brand Architecture

Status: **Canonical v1**

## Brand hierarchy

- **Legal company / owner:** Play Point Systems, LLC
- **Master consumer game brand:** Play Amplified
- **Master consumer domain:** playamplified.com
- **Individual products:** On My List, Chain Reaction, How Close Are We?, The Inside Man, Phone Hold'em, Play Point Trivia, Shot Caddy products during migration, Card Shark, Quest Caddy, Score Caddy, and future games.
- **Music remains separate:** Play Point Records stays under Play Point Systems and is not part of the Play Amplified game experience.

## Core idea

The phone normally competes with the people around it. **Play Amplified reverses that.**

The phone becomes a controller, private information surface, scorekeeper, role holder, card hand, challenge engine, or game layer that creates **more interaction between the people who are already together**.

Play Amplified is not about getting people to spend more time looking at phones. It is about using the phones everyone already has to make the real-world moment more engaging.

## Primary positioning

### PLAY AMPLIFIED
**Phones in the game. People in the moment.**

Supporting explanation:

> Use the phones everyone already has to create more conversation, competition, laughter, strategy, and interaction between the people already together.

## Product promise

Play Amplified can do either of two things:

1. **Start the game.** A normal gathering becomes an interactive social, card, trivia, or group game.
2. **Amplify what is already being played.** A real sport or physical activity keeps happening while Play Amplified adds a second game layer, new decisions, challenges, powers, strategy, scoring, or story.

Examples:

- On My List, Chain Reaction, How Close Are We?, The Inside Man, Phone Hold'em and Trivia **start the game** around a group that is already together.
- Shot Caddy-style experiences **amplify the round** without replacing disc golf or golf.
- Card Shark **amplifies a physical throwing/putting activity** by making the same attempts build a second card game.
- Quest Caddy **amplifies the round into an adventure** while the underlying real-world play continues.

## Brand language

Preferred concepts:

- Phones in the game. People in the moment.
- Turn the phone from a distraction into an engagement tool.
- More interaction between the people already together.
- Start a game or add another layer of play to what you are already doing.
- Amplify the round.
- Amplify the night.
- Amplify the backyard.
- Amplify the adventure.

Avoid positioning Play Amplified as:

- a collection of generic "phone games"
- an experience where the screen is the destination
- a replacement for the sport, gathering, conversation, or physical activity
- a video-game library or traditional mobile-game publisher

The **people and the real-world moment are the experience; the phone facilitates it.**

## Platform structure

The consumer platform should eventually center on:

- **Host a Game**
- **Join a Game**
- **My Games**
- **The Lobby** — internal platform/room language, not the master brand
- **Choose Another Game / Return to the Lobby** after a game ends

Recommended category language:

- Play Together
- Cards & Trivia
- Amplify Your Round
- Play Anywhere / Backyard
- Adventure
- Score / Tools

## Ownership model

The master platform should preserve the simple host/guest model where appropriate:

- One host/owner starts the experience.
- Guests join the room without being presented with room creation on invite/QR entry.
- The group stays central; individual phones provide private information and controls.
- Where practical, a room should be able to move from one Play Amplified game to another without forcing every player to rejoin.

## Migration guardrails

- Do **not** rename individual products solely because the master brand is now Play Amplified.
- Shot Caddy naming is a separate product-level decision and should be handled independently.
- Existing product domains can remain active during migration and later redirect into appropriate PlayAmplified.com routes.
- PlayPointSystems.com remains the corporate/company site.
- PlayAmplified.com becomes the consumer-facing game destination.

## One-sentence definition

**Play Amplified uses the phones people already have to create more interaction with the people they are already with — either by starting a shared game or by adding a new game layer to something they are already playing.**
