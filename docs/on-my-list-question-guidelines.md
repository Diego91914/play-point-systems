# On My List question guidelines

Questions should produce personal, guessable lists rather than objective trivia.

Good prompts:
- are about the Surveyed Player by name;
- let that player sincerely rank their own answers;
- create multiple plausible answers so table knowledge matters;
- are family-safe and conversation-generating;
- remain interesting when reused with a different Surveyed Player;
- avoid generic noun swaps that create fake variety.

Avoid invasive medical, sexual, political, traumatic, or precise financial disclosures. Avoid prompts with one universally obvious list. Prefer specific everyday preferences, habits, travel, food, entertainment, hypotheticals, purchases, annoyances, and activities.
