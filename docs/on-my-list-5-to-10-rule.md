# On My List — Locked Board Size Rule

- The Surveyed Player may enter **5 to 10 ranked answers**.
- Ten answers are encouraged, but never required.
- `SUBMIT BOARD` becomes available once five valid, distinct answers are entered.
- Empty trailing answer slots are ignored.
- Scoring is based on the actual submitted board size: a 10-answer board scores 10 through 1, an 8-answer board scores 8 through 1, and a 5-answer board scores 5 through 1.
- The board should never force filler answers just to reach ten.
