# On My List MVP

Working title: **On My List**.

Launch MVP includes 66 curated list prompts, ranked 5–7 answer boards, randomized guess order, two-miss board elimination, per-answer rank scoring, synchronized reveals, two Surveyed Player boards per person, QR/code joining, individual standings, replay, and server-private unrevealed answers.

The title is intentionally distinct from Family Feud; the game is built around one real person at the table being the survey rather than a population survey.
