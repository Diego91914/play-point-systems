# How to play The Inside Man

## Goal
The Crew tries to complete three missions or expose the hidden Inside Man. The Inside Man tries to cause three sabotage points while staying undiscovered.

## Start
One signed-in player hosts a private room. Everyone else scans the QR code or enters the six-character room code and a first name. The game supports 4–10 players.

## Secret roles
At the start of the match, one phone privately shows **INSIDE MAN**. Everyone else sees **CREW**. Roles remain fixed for the match.

## Missions
Each mission gives the table a situation and six possible choices. Talk face-to-face, persuade each other, then privately choose exactly three options on your phone. The three options with the most support become the group's decision.

Crew members are trying to make the strongest group decision. The Inside Man has a secret sabotage target and tries to get it into the final decision without looking guilty.

## Private objectives
Every player gets a private objective each mission. Crew members may be asked to push one particular useful choice. The Inside Man is asked to push a harmful choice. This means suspicious behavior is evidence—not proof.

Completing a private objective earns an individual side-goal point. These points are bragging rights and do not override the team win.

## Accusations
After a mission, the host may open accusations. Each player has one accusation for the whole match. Passing costs nothing. A clear accusation target with at least two accusers goes to trial.

## Trial
The accused defends themselves aloud. Everyone else privately votes **GUILTY** or **NOT GUILTY**. A majority Guilty vote convicts.

- Convict the real Inside Man: Crew wins immediately.
- Convict an innocent player: Inside Man gains one sabotage point.
- Vote Not Guilty: play continues.

Nobody is eliminated, so everyone stays involved until the end.

## Win
First to three mission points wins the operation. Crew can also win instantly by correctly convicting the Inside Man. If all five missions finish without either side reaching three, the stronger mission record wins; a tie favors the Inside Man for surviving undiscovered.
